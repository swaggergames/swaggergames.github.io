Turbo Tails – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Turbo Tails ("the game") is made by Swagger Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We've kept the game as private as we can: we don't ask for your name or email, there are no accounts, and we don't collect your data on our own servers. The only exception is a problem report that you choose to send (see section 7).

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels completed, hearts, coins, power-ups and settings) and whether you've bought "Remove Ads" are saved only on your phone. We can't see this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows an advert between levels using Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The only thing for sale is a one-off "Remove Ads" purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. Google tells us that a purchase was made so the game can switch adverts off and restore your purchase on a new device.

4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends us a technical report so we can fix it. Reports contain information such as the device model, Android version, game version, the state of the game when the problem happened and an installation identifier. They don't contain your name or contact details.

More information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Google Play Games (optional)

If you use Google Play Games, the game can save your progress to your Play Games account so it survives a new phone. That data is stored by Google under your Google account and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

6. Children

Turbo Tails is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Reporting a problem (optional)

If you use "Report a problem" in the game, the report is sent to our private support desk (a service hosted on Cloudflare) when you press Send. It contains what you write, the type of problem, the game version, your phone model, the level you were playing and your screen size. You can add an email address if you would like a reply; it is used only to reply to that report, is stored encrypted and is shown to us only in masked form. The game also remembers the email address you typed on your phone so you don't have to type it again; it stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. The game also creates a random ID that stays on your phone (it is not linked to you); the support desk uses it, together with a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address for up to 24 hours, only to limit spam, and we do not keep your IP address itself. We keep reports until they have been dealt with and delete them 12 months after they are closed. If the game can't reach the support desk, it offers to write the same details in an email to dev439adem@gmail.com instead; if you use that, your email app and email provider handle the message in the usual way.

8. Your choices

  • Change your ad consent choice from the game's privacy options, or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop adverts entirely.
  • Uninstall the game to delete all data stored on your device.
  • To delete a problem report you sent, email us and tell us roughly when you sent it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy or the game: dev439adem@gmail.com (please put "Turbo Tails support" in the subject).
