GEMSTERS Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-05

GEMSTERS ("the game") is made by Swagger Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. Contact us any time at dev439adem@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, items, currencies, settings) is saved on your device so you can keep playing.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game uses your Play Games profile to back up your progress to Google's Saved Games service and to submit your Rush scores to Google Play leaderboards, where your Play Games name and score are visible to other players. Google's privacy policy applies to this data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation (order/transaction identifier) so we can deliver your items. We never see your payment details.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional reward videos. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Ads are non-personalised unless you consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off interstitial ads. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Gameplay analytics (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Anonymous gameplay analytics" in Settings, the game records anonymous gameplay events (for example level results and which power-ups were used) to help us balance the game. It contains no name, email address or contacts.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset progress to delete progress stored on your device, or uninstall the game. To delete your cloud save, remove the game's data from your Google Play Games account settings. For any other request, email dev439adem@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Virtual items

Gems, tokens and other virtual items have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for money. Purchases are subject to Google Play's refund policy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
